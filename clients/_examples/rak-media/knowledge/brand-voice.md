# Brand voice — RAK Media

## Governing principle

RAK Media carries an emirate's voice. The operator is not a brand performing brand voice; it is an institution with a daily trusted presence. The voice decisions below protect that institutional character even when the surface is commercial.

## Core attributes

- **Institutional, not corporate.** Steady, considered, long-horizon. The operator exists before every launch and will exist after every launch.
- **Arabic-first, multilingual.** Arabic leads editorial. English, Malayalam, Hindi, and Tagalog follow depending on surface and segment. Translations are proper translations, not transliterations.
- **Trusted, not promotional.** The daily trusted presence (radio drive-time, call-ins, local-service announcements) is the operator's primary commercial asset. Promotional voice damages it. Branded content integrates into trust, not around it.
- **Discrete on protocol.** Royal and ministerial references are matter-of-fact. Honorifics correct. No performative deference, no proximity signalling.
- **Specific, not aspirational.** The emirate's stories are concrete: Julphar founded 1980; the Half Marathon women's world record set here in 2020; Stevin Rock quarrying 80 million tonnes a year. Numbers and nouns beat adjectives.
- **Local-first, GCC-fluent, world-aware.** Audience sits in RAK; the vocabulary of the region sits comfortably; the global context is referenced when relevant and never fetishised.

## Surface voice adaptations

| Surface | Voice shape | Example (not literal) |
|---|---|---|
| Radio drive-time | Warm, direct, service-first — "road closure on Al Rams, use the Khor Khwair bypass" | Daily, present-tense |
| Social discovery | Short, newsworthy, Arabic-first with English parallel; no slang beyond regional norm | Headline + attribution |
| YouTube long-form | Journalistic, sourced, archival-grade | "The quarry that supplies the Gulf's roads" |
| Podcast | Conversational depth; interviewer earns the chair | 45-minute interview with a RAK founder |
| Owned digital (Heart of RAK) | Service-oriented, livability-focused, newcomer-friendly | "What to know before your first week in Al Hamra" |

## No-go

- Breathless adjectives (world-class, iconic, world-leading) unless literally sourced and attributed
- Comparative diminishment of Abu Dhabi, Dubai, or any sister emirate — ever
- Perceived endorsement of a political position outside the emirate's declared policy
- Religious-practice commentary beyond what the Ruler's Court or federal authorities have declared
- Advertiser-adjacent language inside Ruler-touching content

## Where editorial meets commercial

A sponsor read is not an editorial segment. A sponsored franchise is not an editorial compromise if the editorial fence is maintained; the fence lives in the franchise charter (`clients/rak-media/commercial/franchises.md`). A sponsor cannot buy voice; a sponsor can buy adjacency to voice, under the rights class permitted by `rights-licensing-coordinator`.

## Review

Every externally-facing artifact routes through `review` (brief + voice) + `compliance` (UAE Media Council, advertising standards) + `rights-licensing-coordinator` (commercial attachment). Protocol-sensitive artifacts additionally route through `government-stakeholder-manager`.
