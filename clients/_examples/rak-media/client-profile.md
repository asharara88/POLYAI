# RAK Media — client profile

## What they are

A government-mandated multi-surface media operator for Ras Al Khaimah. The institutional basis is an emirate-level media law giving the operation legal personality, financial and administrative independence, and a reporting line directly to the Ruler. The remit is emirate-wide media policy and coordination, the Ruler's media, studies and opinion polls, archive, international media relations, and crisis communications.

RAK Media is **not** licensed to broadcast under the institutional entity, but operates through:

- **RAK FM** (radio since 1971/72) — the Gulf's first Malayalam broadcast (1992); multilingual (Arabic, English, Malayalam, Hindi, Tagalog); daily trusted presence
- **Social** (Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn) — discovery and newsworthy short-form
- **YouTube** — searchable archive and long-form
- **Podcast** — repurposed depth and dialogue
- **Owned digital platforms** — Heart of RAK (livability platform for residents, newcomers, people considering a move) and the Ruler's own platforms

## Where they are in their arc

Transitioning from a government communications function (Law of 2018, institutional phase) into a commercial IP + partnership platform ahead of the Wynn Al Marjan opening (guided September 2027; expected to move) and the 2028 hotel wave.

The commercial case has been built:
- 20 named target accounts, 80 behind them (RAKBANK first, hotel wave last)
- 5 revenue lines ranked (Partnerships lead, Spot capped at AED 4-13m)
- 4 operating rules, each answering an ADM/DMI mistake
- AED 21-39m addressable spend from 15 listed/audited target accounts
- Commercial opex envelope: AED 3-6m/yr floor
- 5 observable risks with pre-decided responses

Flow is engaged to operate the commercial side alongside the editorial function.

## What they sell

See `verticals/rak-media/playbook.md` for the full revenue-line taxonomy. The short version:

1. **Partnerships** — six category-exclusive Official Partner positions for the opening-year run-in. New money. Lead.
2. **Sponsorship** — title sponsors on three named franchises + tentpole events.
3. **Franchises** — Destination RAK, Jebel Jais, Heart of RAK extended across all four surfaces.
4. **Spot advertising** — hard-ceiling fourth revenue line (AED 4-13m).
5. **Production services** — small in-house unit; non-conflicting third-party work.

## Who buys

Four tiers of accounts (see `clients/rak-media/commercial/named-accounts.md`):

1. Home accounts in RAK (RAKBANK, RAK Properties, RAK Ceramics, Al Hamra Group, Julphar, Marjan, RAKEZ, RAK FTZ, RAKTDA)
2. National brands with a RAK footprint (buyers out of AD and Dubai)
3. National advertisers (buying Northern Emirates once measured)
4. Partnerships (ADM, DMI, federal, international broadcasters)

## Compliance stance

Government-mandated operator — the mandate is the licence to exist. Every commercial package passes `rights-licensing-coordinator` before it goes external. Ruler-touching content never carries commercial attachment. UAE Media Council licensing currency is tracked monthly.

## Brand voice

Institutional, Arabic-first, trusted, long-term, discrete on protocol. See `clients/rak-media/knowledge/brand-voice.md`.

## Approval-gate detail

- **External commercial proposals** — human CCO signs; `rights-licensing-coordinator` must approve first
- **Ruler-touching content** — `government-stakeholder-manager` approves; CCO co-signs for any publicly-released artifact
- **Below-card discounts** — refused by default; CCO-only override
- **Value-in-kind above 20%** — CCO
- **Franchise launches** — CCO (editorial + commercial sign-off)
- **Sponsor integration on Ruler-touching content** — refused by default; no override
