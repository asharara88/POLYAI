# Pack manifest — `rak-media`

Agents activated when a client declares `pack: rak-media` in `client-profile.md`. Everything outside this list stays dormant for `rak-media` clients.

## Pack agents

### Commercial-sales specialists
- `partnerships-director` — category-exclusive Official Partner positions; buyer discovery across the 20-80 named accounts; position-band pricing against filed marketing spend
- `sponsorship-manager` — title sponsors on franchises and tentpole events; sponsor-renewal cadence
- `ad-sales-director` — card-rate discipline; spot inventory ceiling; Dubai desk oversight; agency brief hand-offs
- `commercial-intelligence-analyst` — signal-to-revenue pipeline from the four surfaces + external market signal (filings, launches, agency moves)

### Content + IP specialists
- `franchise-ip-manager` — owns the three named franchises (Destination RAK, Jebel Jais, Heart of RAK); extends IP across the four surfaces
- `multi-platform-producer` — one engine, one calendar, one rights framework across radio + social + YouTube + podcast; prevents the four surfaces fragmenting
- `rights-licensing-coordinator` — gates what can be commercialised; interfaces with the Ruler's Court on Ruler-touching content; UAE Media Council licensing

### Audience + stakeholder specialists
- `audience-measurement-analyst` — the currency; PwC-verified third-party panels; reach and unduplicated reach across surfaces; audience-quality splits
- `government-stakeholder-manager` — Ruler's Court, GSEC, ministerial, federal counterparts, visiting delegations; protocol-sensitive comms
